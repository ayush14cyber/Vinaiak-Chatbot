###### About

Students’ Gymkhana (SG) aims to provide students a role in the administrative and academic governance of the institute and supports them to develop leadership and administrative skills. We operate in a dynamic manner under the guidance of our faculty advisers.

[Constitution](https://people.iitism.ac.in/~dsw/docs/sgc.pdf)

 

###### Fests

Fests have always been an integral part of college life, and at IIT(ISM) Dhanbad, we organise some of the best technical, cultural and sports fests in the country. They are highly anticipated events across India, with a footfall of over 40000. Students put in their heart and soul into both organising and participating in popular events to make them some of the most memorable experiences.

[Explore Fests](https://sgiitism.in/fests)

###### Technical Clubs

The technical clubs at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad breathe life into the latent potential of its members. With the cutting edge infrastructure improving over the years, the technical clubs have nurtured numerous innovations.

###### Cultural Clubs

The vibrant culture of India is celebrated the world over. We, at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad, strive to uphold this asset through an array of cultural activities conducted by the numerous cultural clubs of IIT (ISM) Dhanbad.
